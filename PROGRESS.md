# GeSignal handoff

## Current stage · GeGI pulse overlap and two-site reconstruction

The GeGI extension is at stage 4 of 5. The current development milestone studies how overlapping pulses affect nominal fast and slow readout. The two-site Control panel shows both energy-based Compton orders for each compatible strip pairing and compares the measured sum with the configured monoenergetic line. A missing spatial coordinate leaves the cone unavailable. The [development plan](tools/NEXT_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) records the next work.

The student command `tools/gegi_pulse_replay.py` places two original nonzero IDs from one completed standalone run on a common 50 MS/s ADC clock. Both pulses share the front-end response, noise stream and quantizer. Coincident rises on the two detector faces provide timing. Each electrode is checked for contamination in its own feature window. The output retains signed ADC samples, trigger times, available energies and position status. Seven pulse-stream regression tests cover single-pulse digital parity, fractional delay, the feedback tail, channel-local overlap and incomplete measurements.

The controlled scan used 12 distinct saved 500 keV charge responses at six pairs of positions. Linear scaling supplied ordered energies of 250/250, 250/125 and 125/250 keV. Two ADC phases, two noise conditions and 19 separations from 0 to 6 µs produced 1,368 paired acquisitions, with 96 isolated and 18 empty controls. At every sampled delay from 0.30 µs onward all three topologies had two timing triggers in 12/12 trials per noise condition. At 0.80 µs the nominal-noise slow-energy counts were 0/12 on the same strips, 6/12 on neighboring strips and 12/12 on distant strips. From 1.00 µs onward all tested pairs yielded both energies. The nearby pair sharing Y accounted for all six energy losses at 0.80 µs. On distant pairs one member lacks a calibrated XY position. The fixed grid measures nominal engineering behavior. Full inputs, seeds and errors are bound in `.local/gegi-double-pulse/scan.json` (SHA-256 `8023fdb9f57b00e756a870925afcaea8f3260c096205f3fbfc70765a78fae0f9`), reproduced with `tools/gegi_pulse_scan.py --output <new-file.json>`.

The first simultaneous-source clock uses the saved left Cs-137 and right Co-60 transport libraries (10,000 parents each) with example activities of 37 kBq per source and a 0.25 s live time. Independent Poisson draws reuse marked parent histories, including misses. Of 419 Cs and 652 Co Ge deposit groups in the equilibrium window, 417 of the Cs groups came from parents before the window. Starting with no pre-existing daughters gave two Cs and 652 Co groups. The equilibrium case has ten cross-source deposit pairs within 6 µs, including one within 0.30 µs. These counts precede electronics. Source settings, seed 20261118, original IDs and timing marks are in `.local/gegi-source-clock/pilot.json`, reproduced by `tools/gegi_source_clock.py --output <new-file.json>`. The transport resets the parent decay clock and retains daughter delays. [remage timing convention](https://remage.readthedocs.io/en/stable/manual/generators.html)

Noiseless ADC replay carried preceding known signals into those ten windows. Seven had sufficiently bounded analog history, all seven had two matched triggers and four retained both qualified energies. Three windows have an incomplete or missing earlier charge response. The closest pair (31.68 ns) in isolation produced one multi-strip trigger. Its full-acquisition outcome remains unknown. With a 6 versus 8 µs digital preamble the common samples and trigger classifications matched on all seven. See `.local/gegi-source-clock/pileup.json` or run `tools/gegi_source_pileup.py --output <new-file.json>`. These selected windows cannot estimate whole-acquisition trigger or energy efficiency.

## Four transport-generated candidates

The 662 keV standalone run had four energy-unique strip pairings. Total deposits in Ge were 470.40, 370.10, 662.00 and 418.13 keV in events 1412, 1575, 2950 and 3472 respectively. Under the full-absorption, stationary-free-electron model, the first three have one allowed order and the last has two. In 2950, digital energy admits only B → A (43.41°). The parent photon's Compton and photoelectric interactions occurred in A → B order. The measurement and the simulated history are shown side by side in the exhibit.

At the eight hit pixels we reused 88 same-field center references and captured 224 full-electrode injections with the original Float32 field. The drift batch took 47.46 s with a 2.48 GB peak working set. In 16 separately composed two-site tests, fitting within the strips changed transverse RMS error from 1.747 to 0.945 mm and depth RMS from 0.237 to 0.106 mm. In event 2950 the fitted points differed from the simulated deposited-energy centroids by 0.45 and 2.49 mm. Both centroids fall outside the ±0.4-pitch training range on one axis. These checks support no validated new imaging cone from the four. Local evidence: `.local/gegi-digital-v1/standalone-order-spectra.json` and `.local/gegi-digital-v1/two-site-position/position-study.json`.

Two separate 5,000-primary left/right source runs yielded 253/231 nonzero primaries, 16/12 energy-unique pairings and 2/7 such pairings with full deposition. One left-source event and three right-source events matched two significant parent interactions in the same strip cells. Both orders passed the energy model in all four matched events. Five fully deposited candidates lacked a scorable two-interaction reference. The full-deposition and reference-match selections follow digital replay. `tools/gegi_two_source_order_check.py --output <new.json>` reproduces `.local/gegi-digital-v1/two-source-order-check.json`.

The nominal electronics use a 50 MS/s digitizer and a fixed pulser calibration. Actual instrument fast-feature and electronics calibration require matched measurements. The published Compton map uses its original reconstruction.
