# GeSignal handoff

## Current stage · continuous GeGI acquisition

Control now accepts two saved GeGI decay libraries for a simultaneous acquisition. Open **Run a dual-source GeGI acquisition**, select the libraries and enter their activities in Bq, the exposure in seconds and a clock seed. Each source keeps its simulated placement. The 34 electrodes share one digitizer clock, with overlapping pulses superposed before noise and quantization. Control displays the spectrum and saves the trigger energies, excluded time and reproducibility receipt. At the 0.25 s reference settings the student workflow reproduced the ADC codes, all 44 time exclusions, every trigger energy and all three histograms from the sealed study. The [student guide](tools/site_guide.html#gegi-dual-source) lists the inputs. The same entry is available as `tools/gegi_dual_source.py`.

At 18.5 kBq Cs-137 and 74 kBq Co-60 over 50 ms, the saved sources at (−40, 0, 40) and (+40, 0, 40) mm contributed 45 and 256 Ge deposit groups. The simulation drew 965 and 3,648 in-window parent decays respectively. Nine groups had incomplete charge and another 23 fell in excluded time. Over 45.276 ms of qualified ADC time, 259 triggers gave 190 energies (113 single-pixel and 77 additional multi-strip). The result assumes an equilibrated parent clock, a reset electronic front end at the start of recording, and the nominal fast/slow transfer functions. The two local receipts are `.local/gegi-student-acquisition/changed-activity.json` and `research-parity.json` in the same folder.

The 0.25 s Cs-137 / Co-60 simulation yielded **713 trigger energies from 864 triggers in 225.318 ms of qualified ADC time**. Each source had an assumed activity of 37 kBq. The single-pixel estimator supplied 484 energies. Summing the measured collecting strips independently on X and Y supplied another 229. The trigger energy is the mean of the two sums when they agree within the existing 2 keV or 2% tolerance. Of 230 multi-strip triggers, one lacked a required Y strip above the collection threshold. The remaining 151 triggers without energy include 142 with guard collection.

Of the 229 additional totals, 227 were uniquely associated with a simulated deposit group for evaluation. All 227 agreed with an isolated waveform control within tolerance. Their RMS differences were 0.046 keV against the isolated measurement and 0.263 keV against simulated deposition. The other two totals belong to merged triggers and enter the recorded spectrum once each. Neither has an individual photon assignment. In a separate noiseless replay of individual groups, all 228 additional multi-strip estimates agreed with deposition within tolerance. The measurement uses ADC data alone. Reproduce the [study](tools/gegi_event_energy_study.py) with `--output .local/gegi-source-clock/<new>.json`; the sealed result is `.local/gegi-source-clock/event-energy.json`.

Control's digital readout panel and the two-event replay command show the trigger energy and both face sums. On saved primary 2950 these were 662.029 keV (X 662.030, Y 662.028). Single-pixel primary 45 retained its original 154.977 keV. In an independent standalone sample, all four previously identified two-site pairing candidates (1412, 1575, 2950 and 3472) produced one trigger total each. The RMS difference from Ge deposition was 0.021 keV for these four noiseless examples. Spatial pairing and interaction order remain separate.

The reset front end sampled 12.5 million times per channel at 50 MS/s. The exposure has 9,360 Cs and 9,184 Co parent decays, with delayed daughters from earlier parents. Of 1,071 Ge deposit groups, 47 lacked complete charge and 113 had uncertain history. Of the 911 evaluable groups, 862 matched unique triggers, four shared two triggers, and 45 were missed. All 45 misses were isolated deposits between 2.57 and 33.83 keV. The clock and noise seeds, exclusions and original associations are in `.local/gegi-source-clock/exposure.json` from `tools/gegi_source_exposure.py`. The original full ADC and trigger pass took 42.5 s locally. The [development plan](tools/NEXT_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) records the next work.

The student command `tools/gegi_pulse_replay.py` places two original nonzero IDs from one completed standalone run on a common 50 MS/s ADC clock. Both pulses share the front-end response, noise stream and quantizer. Coincident rises on the two detector faces provide timing. Each electrode is checked for contamination in its own feature window. The output retains signed ADC samples, trigger times, available energies and position status. Seven pulse-stream regression tests cover single-pulse digital parity, fractional delay, the feedback tail, channel-local overlap and incomplete measurements.

The controlled scan used 12 distinct saved 500 keV charge responses at six pairs of positions. Linear scaling supplied ordered energies of 250/250, 250/125 and 125/250 keV. Two ADC phases, two noise conditions and 19 separations from 0 to 6 µs produced 1,368 paired acquisitions, with 96 isolated and 18 empty controls. At every sampled delay from 0.30 µs onward all three topologies had two timing triggers in 12/12 trials per noise condition. At 0.80 µs the nominal-noise slow-energy counts were 0/12 on the same strips, 6/12 on neighboring strips and 12/12 on distant strips. From 1.00 µs onward all tested pairs yielded both energies. The nearby pair sharing Y accounted for all six energy losses at 0.80 µs. On distant pairs one member lacks a calibrated XY position. The fixed grid measures nominal engineering behavior. Full inputs, seeds and errors are bound in `.local/gegi-double-pulse/scan.json` (SHA-256 `8023fdb9f57b00e756a870925afcaea8f3260c096205f3fbfc70765a78fae0f9`), reproduced with `tools/gegi_pulse_scan.py --output <new-file.json>`.

The first simultaneous-source clock uses the saved left Cs-137 and right Co-60 transport libraries (10,000 parents each) with example activities of 37 kBq per source and a 0.25 s live time. Independent Poisson draws reuse marked parent histories, including misses. Of 419 Cs and 652 Co Ge deposit groups in the equilibrium window, 417 of the Cs groups came from parents before the window. Starting with no pre-existing daughters gave two Cs and 652 Co groups. The equilibrium case has ten cross-source deposit pairs within 6 µs, including one within 0.30 µs. These counts precede electronics. The closest pair (31.68 ns) in isolation produced one multi-strip trigger. Its full-acquisition outcome remains unknown. With a 6 versus 8 µs digital preamble the common samples and trigger classifications matched on all seven. See `.local/gegi-source-clock/pileup.json` or run `tools/gegi_source_pileup.py --output <new-file.json>`. These selected windows cannot estimate whole-acquisition trigger or energy efficiency.

## Four transport-generated candidates

The 662 keV standalone run had four energy-unique strip pairings. Total deposits in Ge were 470.40, 370.10, 662.00 and 418.13 keV in events 1412, 1575, 2950 and 3472 respectively. Under the full-absorption, stationary-free-electron model, the first three have one allowed order and the last has two. In 2950, digital energy admits only B → A (43.41°). The parent photon's Compton and photoelectric interactions occurred in A → B order. The measurement and the simulated history are shown side by side in the exhibit.

At the eight hit pixels we reused 88 same-field center references and captured 224 full-electrode injections with the original Float32 field. The drift batch took 47.46 s with a 2.48 GB peak working set. In 16 separately composed two-site tests, fitting within the strips changed transverse RMS error from 1.747 to 0.945 mm and depth RMS from 0.237 to 0.106 mm. In event 2950 the fitted points differed from the simulated deposited-energy centroids by 0.45 and 2.49 mm. Both centroids fall outside the ±0.4-pitch training range on one axis. These checks support no validated new imaging cone from the four. Local evidence: `.local/gegi-digital-v1/standalone-order-spectra.json` and `.local/gegi-digital-v1/two-site-position/position-study.json`.

Two separate 5,000-primary left/right source runs yielded 253/231 nonzero primaries, 16/12 energy-unique pairings and 2/7 such pairings with full deposition. One left-source event and three right-source events matched two significant parent interactions in the same strip cells. Both orders passed the energy model in all four matched events. Five fully deposited candidates lacked a scorable two-interaction reference. The full-deposition and reference-match selections follow digital replay. `tools/gegi_two_source_order_check.py --output <new.json>` reproduces `.local/gegi-digital-v1/two-source-order-check.json`.

The nominal electronics use a 50 MS/s digitizer and a fixed pulser calibration. Actual instrument fast-feature and electronics calibration require matched measurements. The published Compton map uses its original reconstruction.
