GeGI cryostat assembly

Exact GeGI crystal and all six modeled aluminium walls, reconstructed from the saved Geant4 geometry. Only the frozen GeGI_3D detector is compatible with this cryostat.

Measured front layers; nominal enclosure. The external front is 8.1 mm from the crystal front. The outer and inner front layers are 1.6 mm and 0.8 mm. Side dimensions, rear placement and the split between the front vacuum gaps are explicit modeling assumptions. This is not an as-built instrument or validated efficiency model.

Modeled volumes

The vacuum envelope contains its daughter volumes. Its transparent boundary is not a vacuum occupancy map. The surrounding air world is omitted from the view.

Raw table coordinates are metres and displayed as millimetres. Normalized segment coordinates have explicit mm names. Deposited energy is keV and time is ns. Track PDG numbers identify particles; row indices refer to the original scored table. Exact saved records follow.

Both named aluminium alloys (7075 outer cover, 6061 inner shield) use Geant4 pure aluminium, G4_Al. The inner rear wall has a specified 1.0 mm thickness, with nominal placement. Missing holders, thermal links/cold finger, cooler, instrument body, screws/flanges, source capsule and shielding are not drawn. Finite contact metallisation and dead layers are not included in this radiation geometry; the detector signal model retains its separate electrode definition.

The native overlap check saved with this geometry passed 10,000 samples for each of the eight assembly volumes. That is a sampled geometry check, not mechanical or experimental validation. Curved walls use 64-sided visual tessellation. The exact crystal octagon is derived from the unchanged native solid's bounds and diagonal extent, with native boundary and occupancy checks, avoiding Geant4 Boolean display offsets. Transport uses the exact saved solids.
