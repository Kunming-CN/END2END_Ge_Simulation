Movies slow physical motion, compress long late tails and include a final display plateau. Final recorded values are held constant; this is not extra simulated transport or proof of full collection. Marker sizes are enlarged. The finite cloud starts as 27 weighted packets on 0.10 / 0.20 mm shells.

34-contact strip response

The same event is read out on every electrode. Positive X-face and negative Y-face signals retain their physical polarity. Neighbour strips can show transient signals even when their final collected signal is zero. The gap example divides the cloud between X08 and X09.
