Full saved gallery · Methods and limitations

Numerical/functional checks are not experimental calibration. Unknown responses and incomplete collection must remain distinct from zero deposition.

Control supports the exact GeGI_3D model only in the dedicated GeGI cryostat. It is incompatible with the LBNL assembly. The other detector models cannot use the GeGI cryostat.

Use the GeGI workflow guide for configurable gamma transport, 34-electrode signed charge/readout and signal-derived Compton cones and directional maps. Canonical 92.3 K and −879/0 V remain unchanged. Mechanical approximations and simulated depth calibration do not establish hardware or experimental validation.
