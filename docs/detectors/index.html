Detector shapes and available workflows

HPGe means high-purity germanium, the crystal material. ICPC means inverted coaxial point contact: a small point electrode combined with a coaxial well. A ring-contact detector has a ring-shaped electrode around the crystal. Open a model’s geometry to compare the contact shapes.
