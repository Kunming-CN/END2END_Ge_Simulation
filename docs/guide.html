Using the detector results library

Compute on your own computer

The website remains a precomputed results library. See the shared SSD CPU/GPU runner and Geant4/remage setup. GPU acceleration is optional and applies to potential solves, not every stage. Radiation transport and semiconductor charge physics are separate.

Download original SSD models

Download all 17 model configurations with includes (ZIP). Each detector page also links its exact original YAML and a ZIP containing required includes and metadata. Keep the ZIP folder layout when loading a model.

These are constructive geometry and semiconductor configurations, not CAD/STL files or numerical field caches. See the distribution guide and original hashes, provenance and assumptions. Candidate, scenario and reference labels are preserved; packaging does not establish convergence or experimental validation.

Explore saved SolidStateDetectors.jl results. No installation, account, or connection to the author's computer is needed to use this website.

Browse a detector

Choose a detector on the home page. Each gallery provides geometry and field views, drift movies, comparison figures, event previews, and signal tables. Use the movie controls for playback; select an image to view it at full size.

MP4 links provide videos for presentations. CSV links provide the exported numerical signals. A browser may display a CSV directly rather than download it.

GeGI: interactive strip signals and supplementary results

Open the GeGI strip explorer. Choose an event, press Play or move the time slider, and select individual strips or all 34 channels. The two faces share a common global x-y projection.

Open the supplementary GeGI study for saved fields, weighting potentials, charge/current curves, collection maps, charge sharing, depth, and temperature studies. These earlier notebook results are a separate study; their settings need not match every gallery event.

What is interactive, and what is not?

GeGI strip signals can be explored in the browser. The 3D movies are pre-rendered: they do not provide free camera rotation. Full scene rotation and advanced field inspection remain desktop ParaView features in the original local workspace.

This website does not run Julia, Geant4, or new detector calculations. Desktop .cmd launchers are not web applications. A portable calculation package for students is a later milestone.

Interpret the results carefully

Synthetic single-site and multi-site deposits illustrate detector response; they are not a radioactive-source transport distribution or a Compton reconstruction. Candidate, scenario, and reference labels are retained.

Field lines are not carrier drift paths. A flat display extension is not proof of complete collection. Read the event status, signal conventions, and assumptions before interpreting a curve.

Functional tests and numerical checks do not establish experimental validation. Dimension-based reference models are not calibrated replicas.

Versions and local use

The underlying library is the saved 20260922_suite_v3 run, with later presentation and publication maintenance. Publishing a new website snapshot does not imply that its physics was recalculated.

The snapshot manifest identifies the exact published files by SHA-256. Project development is recorded in the repository's PROGRESS.md.

A downloaded repository can be browsed by opening docs/index.html. Rebuilding numerical results still requires the original computation workspace; that capability is not included in this first release.
