GeSignal · END2END_Ge_Simulation

From a gamma ray
to a measured energy.

Explore how a high-purity germanium (HPGe) detector turns a radiation interaction into an electrical signal — and how that signal becomes an energy measurement.

One physical chain. Three simulation stages.

Conceptual diagram
  1. 01 · Interaction

    Radiation deposits energy

    Geant4 / remage

    Track interactions in the crystal and record where, when and how much energy is deposited.

    Deposited energy · Edep [keV]

  2. 02 · Detector signal

    Charges drift and induce signal

    SolidStateDetectors.jl

    Transport electrons and holes in the electric field and calculate the charge induced on each electrode.

    Electrode charge · Q(t) [fC]

  3. 03 · Measurement

    Electronics reconstruct energy

    Preamplifier → shaper → peak ADC

    Amplify and shape the signal. Convert the digitized peak to energy using a fixed calibration.

    Reconstructed energy · Erec [keV]

Edep is energy left in the crystal. Erec is energy inferred from the readout. Following both reveals how charge collection and electronics affect the measurement. ADC means analog-to-digital converter.
