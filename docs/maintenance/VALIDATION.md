# Validation and interpretation

Each run retains its own JSON reports. `catalog_summary.json` provides a cross-detector overview; `delivery.json` inventories the final deliverables.

1. Cached dissertation and GeGI configurations are compared with a freshly parsed SSD model, including resolved drift-model includes. Model/cache hashes are checked before and after export. New reference models retain their solved fields, model hash, grid dimensions and solver-update checks.
2. VTK coordinate/array ordering is checked at exported interpolation probes. Semiconductor meshes must be closed and manifold; axial polycones also have an analytic-volume comparison. These checks validate export and geometry rendering, not experimental fidelity.
3. Every event checks increasing physical timestamps, finite coordinates, electron-plus-hole signal consistency and raw trajectory containment. Analysis checks no-trapping endpoint identity, exact stochastic seed replay, final source hashes and electronics integration sensitivity.
4. Every active event scene is reloaded. At start, middle and end, displayed electron/hole coordinates, pulse values and the physical clock are compared with the SSD export. The final display extension is checked for constant values and a matching saved playback end. MP4 codec, resolution, frame count and duration are retained in FFprobe reports.

## Limits that remain visible

Random positions are sampled uniformly in Cartesian volume, conditioned on exact semiconductor geometry, the SSD bulk flag, no inactive-layer flag, and six-axis 0.3 mm clearance. Four of the twelve candidates per detector are shown, chosen by spatial coverage rather than waveform quality; all original numerical candidates remain available. They are not sampled from a radioactive source or photon transport. Some original models have low-field regions, incomplete collection, inactive-layer stops or drift-limit endpoints; these remain explicitly identified. An electrode endpoint flag alone does not characterize charge-collection efficiency.

The fixed point is selected from an existing successful bulk scan when available and must contain the full initial cloud. AK02 retains its established point. Random events are separately reproducible and are not selected by signal quality. GeGI random plots select the hole-collecting readout where identifiable; every raw contact signal is still retained.

Cylindrical fields are axisymmetric solutions expanded into 3D for viewing. Cartesian KL01, bipolar and GeGI models retain their full 3D fields. Drift still uses the original SSD mobility model and its crystal anisotropy. Display grids are sampled at 0.35 or 1 mm as recorded in fields.json; the solver and carrier drift use their original finer grids. VTK field lines terminate at the sampled interior mask and are not drift paths. Outside-crystal interpolation values have no physical interpretation and are excluded from plots.

Source native-grid mesh convergence, domain convergence and experimental calibration are not established by this library. Solver update tolerances are convergence monitors, not guaranteed physical error bounds. Recorded time-step, seed and packet-number differences should be inspected before drawing quantitative conclusions. A small final-charge difference does not establish a converged instantaneous-current peak. Fixed-point time-step tests are not a detector-wide convergence study.

Diffusion and self-repulsion are SSD packet models. The cloud has 27 weighted packets for the production examples and 52 for the larger packet check; it does not track every microscopic carrier. Stopped markers remain visible and marker radii are display-only. Full raw trajectories remain in event.raw.jls; display JSON retains all early samples and samples long late tails more coarsely. Motion between saved samples is interpolated. Signal CSV retains the regular SSD waveform grid. Revision 3 movies include the full recorded interval with nonuniform playback: early motion is emphasized and long late tails are compressed. A final hold of 20% of recorded duration (at least 10 ns) makes the end visible in both scenes and plots. This is a display extension, not additional charge transport. Incomplete events retain their final values and explicit warning; their charge is never forced to one.

Each detector presents five two-site and three three-site examples spanning spatial separation, depth and equal/unequal energy sharing. All deposits are simultaneous and total 662 keV. These are controlled input examples, not simulated gamma interactions or Compton events. Revision 3 adds 119 SSD simulations and exposes 276 scenes across 17 models, with four MP4 and WebM clips per model.

The electronics figures show a noiseless illustrative charge-sensitive preamplifier and CR-RC shaping chain, with 50 us decay and 0.5 us shaping. They are not fitted to measured electronics. The large reference models idealize contacts, impurity density and unreported details. The GeGI position illustration uses the strongest orthogonal strips, not a calibrated reconstruction or Compton algorithm.

For GeGI the 34 signed channels sum to zero after full collection, but their transient sum is not identically zero: the largest absolute sum in this event library is about 0.026 of deposited-energy normalization. On the display grid the sum of all 34 weighting potentials spans about 0.919 to 1.000 inside the crystal. The contacted faces do not enclose the whole crystal: the model includes uncovered surfaces, vacuum and infinity boundaries. The transient sum is retained without artificial renormalization; this check does not establish convergence of edge fields.

The gallery filter, navigation and layout were checked in the embedded app browser. That browser crashed when activating video playback with both MP4 and WebM; playback inside that app could not be certified. Media files are independently checked by full FFmpeg decoding. Open `Open_Library.cmd` in the system browser, or use the MP4 files in a video player/PowerPoint. Both common slide-ready MP4 and open-codec WebM preview files are retained.

Revision 4 separates pulse detail from full recorded history when long low-amplitude tails would compress the main pulse. The focus window is derived from cumulative absolute variation of all relevant signed curves, not from a forced final charge of one. Native reload checks verify both horizontal ranges, vertical bounds, external legend locations, and the original signal values. No signal is discarded from the full-history panel or the numerical exports. Comparison plots explicitly show pulse detail; their original CSV and event histories remain available.
