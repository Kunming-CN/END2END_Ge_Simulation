Simulation methods

How radiation deposits become electrode signals, readout energies and interaction candidates.

Radiation transport

Geant4/remage records deposited energy Edep in keV, positions in mm, creation and deposition times in ns, and original primary and track identities. A primary may miss the crystal, interact more than once or produce delayed daughter deposits. Keep relevant deposits of one primary and pulse group together.

Charge transport and induction

SolidStateDetectors.jl transports electrons and holes through the detector electric field. Electrode weighting potentials determine signed induced charge through the Shockley–Ramo relation. Charge in fC and current in nA belong to this stage; deposition time is not carrier drift time. Incomplete collection, guard contacts and numerical termination flags stay with the response.

Two readout branches

LBNL analog electronics

Signed current passes through fixed wiring, a preamplifier and analog shaper. A peak ADC (analog-to-digital converter) records one peak code. The analog numerical time grid is separate from a waveform digitizer.

GeGI digitized strip readout

The nominal digital branch samples preamplifier voltages on 34 electrodes. Fast neighboring-strip responses inform position, while the slow branch measures energy. X and Y are two measurements of the same deposited charge; their energies are not added.

Shared-readout studies combine overlapping signals before common noise, digitization and triggering. Their source history, clock organization and reset conditions belong to each study.

Voltages are in V and reconstructed energy Erec is in keV. Independent synthetic injections provide a fixed calibration shared across events. Gain is never chosen separately to match each event's Edep. Thresholds, clipping or rejection can leave energy unavailable; an unknown response is not zero.

Interaction candidates and Compton imaging

A two-strip trigger may admit multiple pairings and interaction orders. Permitted Compton cones retain their assigned weights. A source-plane reconstruction then combines them under a stated spatial prior. Uniform area gives equal cells equal initial weight; uniform solid angle weights their angular area from the detector center. Weight without a usable projection remains unresolved.

Signal-derived positions and source truth have separate roles. Programmed source coordinates may evaluate a reconstructed peak; they do not choose measured candidates. Study pages retain the precise calibration, candidate policy, numerical budget and validation limits.

Counts, identity and interpretation

Initial primaries or decays, positive-deposit primaries, pulse groups, accepted energies and candidate orders are different populations. Each plot names its denominator and selection. An event number identifies a record together with its study, run, model variant and pulse group. Zero deposits, native failures, electronics rejections and unresolved imaging weight remain distinct.

Engineering completion, numerical convergence and agreement with measurement are separate conclusions. Plot rounding does not alter stored numbers or histogram bins. Exact settings, seeds, units, failure/null accounting and downloadable records remain in each study.
