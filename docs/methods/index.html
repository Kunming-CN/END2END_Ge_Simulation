Principles, assumptions and limits

Methods & limitations

The radiation, semiconductor and electronics stages retain distinct quantities and identities. These saved engineering examples do not establish experimental agreement or calibrated charge-collection efficiency.

Radiation deposits

Geant4/remage tracks the radiation and records deposited energy Edep in keV, positions in mm, creation/deposition times in ns and original primary/track identities. A primary may miss the crystal, interact more than once or produce delayed daughter deposits. Keep deposits belonging to one primary and pulse group together.

Semiconductor charge and induction

SolidStateDetectors.jl transports the generated electrons and holes in the detector electric field. Electrode weighting potentials determine the signed induced charge through the Shockley–Ramo relation. Charge in fC and current in nA belong to this stage; deposition time is not carrier drift time. Incomplete collection, guards and numerical termination flags remain part of the result.

Electronics and reconstructed energy

A fixed wiring convention passes the signed current to a preamplifier, analog shaper and peak ADC. Voltages are in V. Reconstructed energy Erec in keV uses an independent injection calibration shared across events, never an event-by-event match to Edep. Threshold, clipping and rejection can make energy unavailable; an unknown response is not zero.

Counts, identity and validation

Initial primaries or decays, nonzero primaries, pulse groups and accepted readout groups are distinct populations. Each plot names its denominator and selection. Event numbers only identify a record together with its study, independent run, model/variant and pulse group. Engineering completion, numerical convergence and agreement with measurement are separate claims; these examples are not measured detector calibration.

Plot display rounding does not change stored values or histogram bins. Each study retains exact settings, seeds, failure/null accounting and downloadable records.
