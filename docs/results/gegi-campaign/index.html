Study design and saved populations

Four independent acquisitions: two 662 keV monoenergetic photon runs of 200,000 initial primaries each, plus Cs-137 and Co-60 runs of 300,000 initial records each. The combined count is 1,000,000 initial records.

The current conditional image uses 434 signal-selected groups and 837 candidate orders. Its 40 iterations reached the saved budget; convergence remains unestablished. The separate historical control contains 708 groups.

Study setup: GeGI_3D detector model · GeGI cryostat assembly · Readout candidates and Compton imaging method.

Selection, uncertainty and saved evidence

All 434 selected groups contribute 837 candidates. The original acquisition retains 1,000,000 initial records and 57,613 nonzero pulse groups. Unsupported site orders retain unresolved weight; surviving candidates are not renormalized to a full event.

The saved conditional fit stopped at 40 iterations; convergence has not been established. Its finite angular response uses the saved position/discrepancy model. Source distance and source count remain unidentified; event recovery and laboratory resolution are not validated by these figures.
