Exact classification evidence


“Full” means a numerical match to ONE ancestor photon’s birth energy in one saved 100 µs window, using max(10⁻⁵ keV, 10⁻⁸ Eγ). It does not mean every Cs137 decay emits 662 keV.


Compton counts are observed electron-creation sites on photon ancestry. Same-vertex/time secondary electrons share one site. Step counts are not scatter counts. Without per-step processes/energies, precise interaction multiplicity is unresolved.


Compact SSE candidates have a maximum positive-deposit diameter ≤ 1 mm. This is a spatial screen, not calibrated PSD. Partial-energy cases do not establish escape or a complete energy balance. Categories may overlap; counts refer to pulse groups.


Representative buttons prefer the highest observed photon-energy family, then its median deposit diameter (compact) or Ge energy. This is illustrative, not statistical typicality. Empty categories stay empty; no substitution. The button reports the actual photon energy.
