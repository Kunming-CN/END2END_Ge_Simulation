## Shared world, assets and placements

Keep versioned ASSETS separate from placed INSTANCES and backend adapters. Cryostat
assets provide inspected dimensions/materials/placements. Detector assets provide
canonical geometry, contacts, impurity and bias; instances supply the coordinate
transform and Geant4-sensitive-volume/SSD/readout mapping. Source definitions own
particle/isotope identity, correlated decay/spectrum, timing, units, normalization
and spatial policy; source pose owns position and orientation.

One shared resolver supplies checked effective inputs to geometry, source macro,
CLI, Control and provenance. Existing fixed-pose contracts and receipts remain
readable and strict. A new pose needs an explicit supported version; do not rebase
old evidence. Preserve the exact zero-angle world and old Gamma defaults. Moving
the radioactive point also moves its capsule/fill and their own rotation, leaving
crystal/spacer untouched. A preview must show the resolved selection and is not
physical containment, overlap or transport acceptance.

Require explicit units/frames, rotations, containment/overlap, contact/readout and
capability checks. Enable a source/model from checked descriptors, not its label.
Preflight returns supported/unsupported reasons, dependencies, resolved settings,
planned stages, reuse decisions and resource bounds. Each physical correction has
one owner to avoid double charge/dead-layer/noise corrections.
Register supported assets through the existing catalog/common functions. Do not
maintain one configuration/geometry for every combination or invent a bespoke
adapter for every detector. Run-local resolved input snapshots are still required
for traceability. Arbitrary uploaded geometry/physics is not promised.

## Runtime, recovery and analysis contracts

Backend state and committed stage/group records govern queued, running, stop,
failed and complete states. A zero exit code alone is not completion. Stop retains
partial evidence; Resume checks exact source/configuration/pose provenance. Show
known progress, explicit resource limits and meaningful errors, not invented ETA.

Keep all initial events including zeros, every deposit in its proper event/group,
creation times distinct from drift times, and truth Edep distinct from readout Erec.
Retain signed signals, native-failure nulls, caps/rejection flags and independent
injection calibration. A readout threshold changes validity, not the event census.
Saved radiation, ledgers, charge, readout and settings remain accessible through
existing read-only/lazy routes; selected IDs/files/plots must agree when refreshed.

Preserve loopback-only service, token/origin/CSRF protection, structured arguments,
safe file roots, one-writer locks and no executable uploads or silent installations.
Browser acceptance never clicks Download/Save As or changes owner browser settings;
protected HTTP/source checks verify exact downloadable bytes separately.
