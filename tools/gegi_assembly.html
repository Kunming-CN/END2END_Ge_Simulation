Both named aluminium alloys (7075 outer cover, 6061 inner shield) use Geant4 pure aluminium, G4_Al. The inner rear wall has a specified 1.0 mm thickness, with nominal placement. Missing holders, thermal links/cold finger, cooler, instrument body, screws/flanges, source capsule and shielding are not drawn. Finite contact metallisation and dead layers are not included in this radiation geometry; the detector signal model retains its separate electrode definition.

The native overlap check saved with this geometry passed 10,000 samples for each of the eight assembly volumes. That is a sampled geometry check, not mechanical or experimental validation. Curved walls use 64-sided visual tessellation. The exact crystal octagon is derived from the unchanged native solid's bounds and diagonal extent, with native boundary and occupancy checks, avoiding Geant4 Boolean display offsets.
