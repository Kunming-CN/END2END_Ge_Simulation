        Normalized deposits use energy_keV in keV, detector-local position_mm in mm and global_position_m in m. In raw LH5, position columns use m, step edep uses keV and time uses ns; track and particle ekin use MeV. Check each raw column’s units in manifest.json → raw_tables → columns; those units are authoritative. The manifest also records the coordinate transform. Pulse plots use time since pulse-group origin in ns. Deposition time, particle creation time and carrier drift time describe different stages.

            Input locations and reconstruction assumptions
            
        Choose a completed standalone GeGI run or a campaign with sealed SQLite signal stores. Reprocessing reads saved signals and fixed readout values into a separate derivative. It starts no radiation, carrier drift, field solve or calibration.
        
            
        
              Completed GeGI runs owned by Control appear automatically. Imported campaign folders belong in
              .local/gegi-saved-inputs/campaigns/NAME/
              ; independent saved template folders belong in
              .local/gegi-saved-inputs/calibrations/NAME/
              . Existing project dataset/campaign folders and their saved calibration siblings are also offered. Standalone reprocessing has no checkpoint boundary, so Stop and Resume are unavailable; campaign processing retains its chunk boundaries.
            
        
            
        The response model is approximate. Full 34-channel independent templates are not generally available; unsupported signals may produce no supported 3D hypotheses. Conditional candidates are not validated recovered events, and no source location is supplied as the answer. Older saved maps using terminal-CFD timing and strict two-by-two strip selection remain experimental legacy results.
