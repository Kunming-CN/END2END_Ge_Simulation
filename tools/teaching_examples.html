How to interpret these examples
The nominal cryostat and source arrangement are engineering inputs, not a surveyed replica. Native transport includes finite parcel counts, caps and recorded failures. The electronics is synthetic and noiseless; these curves are not measured pulse comparisons or physical energy-resolution predictions.
Original signs, IDs, time origins, deposit rows and flags are retained. The complete 10K event ledgers, zeros and statistical distributions stay in the linked case records. GeRC02 here uses the saved Li 50 minute run variant; its catalog model retains the original 30 minute input. KM is a candidate with fixed readout wiring.
The earlier 200-event bare-gamma teaching dataset is retained as the original teaching archive.
